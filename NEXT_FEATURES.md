# Next Gemma AI Features for Hackathon

## 1. AI Recipe Generator (Recettes.jsx)
Dynamic AI-generated recipes from user's ingredients + health profile.
- Input ingredients + restrictions → Gemma generates complete recipe
- Shows instructions, nutrition, prep time
- Tailored to pathologies, allergies, cal goals

## 2. AI Meal Plan Generator (MealPlan.jsx)
One-click weekly meal plan via Gemma.
- Auto-fills 7-day grid with breakfast/lunch/dinner/snack
- Respects calories, restrictions, pathologies

## 3. AI Barcode Analysis (Barcode.jsx)
Scan barcode → Open Food Facts → Gemma analyzes ingredients.
- "Is this product healthy for your diabetes?"
- Explains ingredients in plain language

## 4. Weekly AI Insights (Dashboard)
Gemma analyzes meal history from Firestore.
- Trend detection: "3 high-sodium meals detected"
- Scores and personalized suggestions
